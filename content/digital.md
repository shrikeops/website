# Digital

## The threat

AI has collapsed the cost of finding vulnerabilities. A [Cloud Security Alliance research note](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-ai-autonomous-vuln-discovery-economics-202/) reports an AI agent finding 21 zero-days in FFmpeg for about $1,000 of compute, and WordPress plugin vulnerabilities for about $20 each. Attacking a small organization is now cheap.

State actors are embedding themselves in low-priority infrastructure ahead of need. The [CISA advisory on Volt Typhoon](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa24-038a) documents a Chinese state actor holding quiet access inside US critical infrastructure networks, positioned to activate during a conventional attack or a natural disaster.

A school, a fire department, a town council: each is an unattractive target on its own. Attacked together, they are worth the effort. The attacker is region-agnostic, so every town is in the target set.

The war in Ukraine has shown how exposed civilian and infrastructure targets are. Canada, the US, and their allies are not prepared for this class of attack.
