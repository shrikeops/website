# About

The attack surface has grown faster than the ability to defend it. Commoditized drones and AI inference have made physical and cyber attacks cheap. Targets like power grids, water systems, and municipal services, once protected by distance and obscurity, are now exposed.

Detection is the first step in hardening infrastructure. We're currently working on acoustic drone detection in noisy soundscapes, and on automated penetration testing and reporting.

## The company

Shrike Ops is 1000033630 Ontario Inc., a Canadian company based in Belleville, Ontario.

It was founded by Greg Clarke, a Canadian-Australian who served in the Australian Army with NATO forces in Afghanistan.

## What is a shrike

Shrikes are pretty little songbirds known for impaling their prey. They are patient hunters.
