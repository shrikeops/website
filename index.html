Shrike Ops

Counter-drone technology

Shrike Ops builds acoustic sensors for directional drone detection.

01

The problem

Drones are cheap and dangerous. A $1,000 drone can cause millions of dollars in damage.

Current acoustic detection methods struggle in noisy soundscapes like airports and live events.

02

What we are building

Directional acoustic drone detection that works in noisy soundscapes.

By combining hardware and software filtering, our microphones can passively identify drone targets at greater distances than current options.

03

Why acoustic

Detection methods like radar are expensive and easy targets. Many of the drone breakthroughs in Ukraine have been achieved by first taking out the radars and then exploiting the opening.

Acoustic sensors are passive. They don't advertise to the enemy where you are and what you want to protect.

04

The company

05

The roadmap

The goal is air littoral dominance. It comes in two parts.

Part one is detection: find the drones. That is the work now.

Part two is interception: safely disable the drones. That work follows.
